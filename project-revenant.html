Air-cooled 45° V-Twin, 4-stroke

OHV, 2 valves per cylinder, pushrod

IGNITION

LUBRICATION
Dry sump, 4.2 L capacity

05 // COMPATIBILITY

PLATFORM
INTEGRATION

The Revenant powertrain is designed as a modular drop-in unit for custom hardtail and rigid frames. Below are verified platform fits and estimated fabrication requirements.

HARLEY-DAVIDSON

Direct fit for Evolution Big Twin frames (1984–1999). Requires custom engine mounts and oil tank relocation for rigid conversions.

COMPATIBLE MODELS
FXST Softail FLST Heritage FXR XL Sportster
EXAMPLE BUILD

1997 FXST — 980cc stroker, hardtail rear section, midsized fuel tank, raw titanium exhaust. Est. build time: 14 weeks.

JAPANESE CRUISER

Adaptable to Yamaha Virago / XV series and Suzuki Intruder frames. Requires custom fab: motor mount brackets, oil line re-routing, and carburetor clearance check.

COMPATIBLE MODELS
Yamaha XV1100 Yamaha XV535 Suzuki VS800 Kawasaki VN900
EXAMPLE BUILD

1992 Yamaha XV1100 — 980cc transplant, full frame chop, custom subframe, Mikuni HSR42 carb swap. Est. build time: 10 weeks.

CUSTOM RIGID

Purpose-built for scratch-built rigid frames using standard 1.25" O.D. tube. Engine acts as a stressed member; no downtubes required.

COMPATIBLE MODELS
Dutchman Rigid PAIN Kustom TC Bros hardtail Biltwell forged
EXAMPLE BUILD

Scratch-built rigid — 51mm front forks, 21" / 18" wheels, 980cc powerplant, side-mount oil tank, no front fender. Est. build time: 18 weeks.
