THE IRON

Rp 425.000.000

A brutalist reinterpretation of the 1978 classic frame. Stripped, reinforced, and engineered for uncompromising urban dominance.

THE STORY

The Iron Shadow began as a wreck salvaged from a shipyard in Hamburg. Our vision was to create a machine that felt like a monolithic extension of the city's concrete architecture.

Every weld was left exposed, sandblasted back to a raw finish. The frame was shortened by 4.5 inches to aggressive the stance, while the custom-fabricated tank was formed from aerospace-grade carbon fiber to reduce weight by 12kg.

This isn't just a motorcycle; it's a technical statement. We prioritized structural honesty over decorative flair—if a part didn't serve a functional purpose, it was removed. The result is a narrow, high-torque beast designed for high-speed lane splitting and absolute mechanical reliability.
