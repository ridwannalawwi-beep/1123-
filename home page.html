01 // IDENTITY

THE MANIFESTO

"In a world drowning in plastic disposables and digital transience, we choose the permanence of cold steel and the honesty of a raw weld."

1123 Workshop is founded on the principle of mechanical honesty. We strip away the unnecessary, leaving only what is required to move soul and machine forward. Every bolt is torqued with intent; every line is drawn to defy trends.

Our process is iterative and destructive. We break things to understand their limits, then rebuild them to surpass those limits. Carbon fiber for strength, chrome for the light, and fire for the spirit.
